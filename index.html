Our Core Capabilities

Comprehensive Trade Services

From farm-gate sourcing to destination port clearance, we provide end-to-end operational, regulatory, and supply chain solutions for global trade.

🌱

Direct Agricultural & Commodity Sourcing

Direct procurement from verified farmers and production mills across India, eliminating multi-tier brokerage markups and ensuring complete crop traceability.

  • ✓ Farm-gate harvest inspection
  • ✓ Verified producer pricing models
  • ✓ Seasonal crop availability tracking
🔬

Quality Inspection & Lab Testing

Pre-shipment sampling and testing via NABL accredited laboratories for chemical residue, moisture levels, microbial count, and destination purity compliance.

  • ✓ Certificate of Analysis (CoA) generation
  • ✓ FSSAI, APEDA & global compliance checks
  • ✓ Express sample dispatch to overseas buyers
✈️

Air & Ocean Freight Logistics

Multi-modal cargo movement covering direct flight booking for perishable greens and ocean container freight (FCL/LCL) for bulk grains and commodity consignments.

  • ✓ Same-day flight booking for fresh produce
  • ✓ Temperature-monitored reefer containers
  • ✓ Port-to-port and door delivery dispatch
📦

Custom Packaging & Cold-Chain

Export-grade packing protocols designed to preserve shelf-life, prevent transit damage, and satisfy international biosecurity standards.

  • ✓ 5-ply export corrugated cartons & vacuum bags
  • ✓ ISPM-15 heat-treated fumigated pallets
  • ✓ GS1 barcoding and customized buyer labeling
📑

Customs Clearances & ICEGATE

End-to-end clearance documentation, ITC (HS) code classification, electronic shipping bill generation on ICEGATE, and PQIS phytosanitary certificate issuance.

  • ✓ Electronic shipping bill execution
  • ✓ Plant quarantine & PSC clearance filings
  • ✓ AD code registration & port documentation
🛡️

DGFT, GST Clearing & Marine Cover

Comprehensive regulatory advisory for SMEs entering foreign trade: DGFT portal management, export GST notice resolutions, and open marine transit insurance covers.

  • ✓ DGFT IEC management & export incentives
  • ✓ GST refund error matching & notice clearance
  • ✓ Institute Cargo Clauses (ICC-A) insurance
